About Velvet Crumbs

Home-baked luxury, crafted in small batches

Velvet Crumbs was born from a love for slow baking, delicate details, and the joy of celebrations. Every cake is baked to order from our home kitchen in Trivandrum, using carefully chosen ingredients and hand-finished designs.

Whether it’s a first birthday, a pastel wedding, or a quiet celebration at home, we work closely with you to bring your ideas to life — from flavours and fillings to colours and textures.
